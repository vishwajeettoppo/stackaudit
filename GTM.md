# StackAudit Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
**Objective:** Establish StackAudit as the essential "Financial Intelligence Layer" for startups, helping teams reclaim 15–30% of their wasted AI budget through automated audits and continuous seat optimization.

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary: The Efficiency-First Founder & CTO
* **Segment:** Seed to Series B startups (10–100 employees).
* **Pain Point:** "Shadow AI" — decentralized teams subscribing to multiple tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Midjourney) on individual cards without oversight.
* **Spend Profile:** $1,500 – $10,000/month across fragmented AI providers.

### Secondary: Boutique AI Agencies & Freelancers
* **Segment:** Agencies managing dozens of client-billable AI seats.
* **Pain Point:** Margin erosion due to unmanaged seat-based subscriptions across diverse projects.

---

## 3. Multi-Channel Execution Strategy

### Phase 1: High-Velocity Launch (Product Hunt)
* **The "Golden Window":** Launch Tuesday at 12:01 AM PST to maximize exposure to the weekly leaderboard.
* **The Maker Network:** Engagement of 15+ previous "Top 10" Makers for feedback and visibility 48 hours prior.
* **The Hook:** A 45-second demo showing "The 60-Second StackAudit"—instantly finding $500+ in wasted subscriptions.
* **Lead Magnet:** "First 100 hunters get 3 months of StackAudit Pro free."

### Phase 2: Community-Led Growth (Reddit & Indie Hackers)
* **Reddit (r/SaaS, r/Startups, r/OpenAI):**
    * **The Strategy:** Post a "Data Study" rather than a pitch. Title: *"I audited 50 startups' AI spend—here are the top 3 tools being overpaid for."*
    * **The Hook:** Provide the value in the body; mention StackAudit as the source of the data in a low-friction way.
* **Indie Hackers:** * **The Breakdown:** *"How I handled OAuth/API integration for 15+ different AI billing portals in 7 days."* This builds authority among technical users.

### Phase 3: Targeted Outbound (Apollo.io & Cold Email)
* **Filter Logic:** Target Series A companies with "Head of Finance," "CTO," or "Operations" titles.
* **The "Audit-First" Subject Line:** *Your StackAudit for [Company Name]*
* **The Hook:** *"I noticed your team is scaling fast. Most teams your size lose 20% of their AI budget to overlapping 'Plus' vs 'API' seats. Can I send you a 2-minute audit?"*

### Phase 4: Social Listening (X/Twitter)
* **The Strategy:** Monitor keywords: "OpenAI bill," "Claude Pro seat management," or "SaaS bloat."
* **Action:** Reply with value-added screenshots comparing pricing tiers (e.g., "Actually, for 5+ people, the Team plan saves you $40/mo compared to individual Pro seats—StackAudit found this in 10 seconds").
