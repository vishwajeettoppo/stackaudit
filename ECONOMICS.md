## 2. Conversion Funnel (The "StackAudit" Leakage Map)



### Stage 1: Awareness (Top of Funnel)
* **Goal:** 10,000 monthly unique visitors (MUVs).
* **Source:** PH Launch, Reddit data posts, Twitter "bill-shaming" threads.

### Stage 2: Engagement (The Free Audit)
* **Action:** User connects 1st integration (e.g., Stripe or Gmail).
* **Benchmark:** 15% conversion from visitor to "Free Audit."
* **Target:** 1,500 Audits/mo.

### Stage 3: Activation (The Aha! Moment)
* **Action:** User sees a potential saving of >$100/mo in the dashboard.
* **Benchmark:** 40% of audits must find "Ghost Subs" or "Zombie Seats."
* **Target:** 600 High-Intent Leads.

### Stage 4: Conversion (Paid Subscription)
* **Action:** User upgrades to "Pro" for continuous monitoring & Slack alerts.
* **Benchmark:** 5% conversion from High-Intent Leads.
* **Target:** 30 New Paid Users/mo.

### Stage 5: Retention (LTV)
* **Metric:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR).
* **Target:** 90% monthly retention.
* **Est. LTV:** $49 * 10 months = **$490 per customer.**

---

## 3. Key Efficiency Metrics
* **LTV/CAC Ratio:** $490 / $26.78 = **18.3x** (Excellent for early-stage SaaS).
* **Payback Period:** < 1 month (Customers cover their acquisition cost in the first payment).
* **Burn Multiple:** Aiming for 1.1x (Every $1 spent on marketing generates $0.90+ in ARR).
